# Aurora Nordic Trading ApS — Purchasing and Supplier Invoice Policy

> **FICTIONAL DEMO POLICY.** Aurora Nordic Trading ApS is a fictional company. This document exists
> only to demonstrate InvoiceIQ. It is not accounting, tax or legal advice and does not describe
> the rules of any real organisation or jurisdiction.

Section identifiers (for example PP-3.1) are stable and are used as citations.

## 1. Purpose and scope

### 1.1 Purpose
This purchasing policy defines how Aurora Nordic Trading ApS receives, checks, approves and pays
supplier invoices. Its goal is to make sure that every purchase is authorised, correctly invoiced
and paid only once, and that purchasing decisions can be audited.

### 1.2 Scope
The policy applies to all supplier invoices for goods and services purchased by any department or
cost center. It applies regardless of whether a purchase order was raised. Employee expense claims
and payroll are out of scope.

### 1.3 Demo disclaimer
All thresholds, VAT rates and tolerances in this policy are fictional demo rules. They must not be
treated as universal accounting practice or tax guidance.

## 2. Invoice content requirements

### 2.1 Mandatory invoice fields
Every supplier invoice must contain: an invoice number, the vendor (supplier) name, the issue date,
the invoice currency, at least one line item, the subtotal (net amount excluding VAT), the VAT rate,
the VAT amount and the total amount payable. An invoice that is missing a mandatory field must not
be paid. Accounts payable returns it to the supplier and requests a corrected invoice.

### 2.2 Line item requirements
Each invoice line item must state a description of the goods or services, the quantity, the unit
price and the line total. Lines without a quantity or unit price cannot be verified and must be
clarified with the supplier before payment.

### 2.3 Invoice currency
The base currency of Aurora Nordic Trading ApS is Danish kroner (DKK). All approval thresholds are
expressed in DKK. Invoices issued in another currency must be converted using the exchange rate
published by Treasury. Accounts payable staff must not estimate exchange rates; such invoices are
routed to Treasury for review before approval thresholds are evaluated.

### 2.4 Purchase order reference
Suppliers should quote an Aurora purchase order (PO) number on every invoice. A purchase order
reference makes it easier to match the invoice with the original purchasing decision, but it does
not replace the approval required by section 3.

## 3. Approval thresholds

### 3.1 Manager approval threshold
Any invoice with a net amount (excluding VAT) above DKK 20,000.00 requires documented approval by
the manager of the responsible cost center before payment. An invoice with a net amount of exactly
DKK 20,000.00 does not require manager approval. The net amount used for this purchasing threshold
is the amount calculated from the invoice line items, not the amount stated by the supplier.

### 3.2 Director approval threshold
Any invoice with a net amount (excluding VAT) above DKK 100,000.00 requires documented approval by a
director or the CFO. Manager approval alone is not sufficient for invoices above this threshold.

### 3.3 Approval evidence
An approval record must name the approver, the approver's role and the approval date. Verbal or
informal approvals are not accepted. An approval may be recorded after the invoice is received, but
it must be documented before the invoice is released for payment.

### 3.4 Missing or insufficient approval
If a required approval is missing or was given by someone without sufficient authority, the invoice
must be placed on payment hold. Accounts payable notifies the budget owner of the cost center and
requests approval at the correct level. The invoice is not paid until a valid approval is recorded.

### 3.5 Split purchases
Splitting a purchase across several invoices or purchase orders to stay below an approval threshold
is prohibited. Suspected split purchases are reported to the finance controller.

## 4. VAT and invoice arithmetic

### 4.1 Standard domestic VAT rate
For domestic purchases (a Danish supplier invoicing Aurora Nordic Trading ApS in Denmark) the
standard VAT rate in this fictional policy is 25% of the net amount. An invoice from a domestic
supplier that applies a different VAT rate requires review by the finance team before payment.

### 4.2 Calculation method
Invoice amounts are verified as follows. Each line total equals quantity multiplied by unit price,
rounded to two decimal places using round-half-up. The subtotal equals the sum of the line totals.
The VAT amount equals the subtotal multiplied by the VAT rate, rounded to two decimal places using
round-half-up. The total amount payable equals the subtotal plus the VAT amount.

### 4.3 Rounding tolerance
A stated amount (line total, subtotal, VAT amount or total) is accepted if it differs from the
calculated amount by no more than DKK 0.01. Any larger difference is an arithmetic discrepancy and
is handled according to section 5.1.

### 4.4 Foreign suppliers
VAT treatment of invoices from foreign suppliers (for example reverse-charge arrangements) is
outside the scope of automated validation. Such invoices are reviewed manually by the tax team.

## 5. Discrepancies and supplier communication

### 5.1 Handling discrepancies
When a stated invoice amount does not match the calculated amount, accounts payable places the
invoice on payment hold. Staff must not correct amounts on the supplier's behalf or pay a different
amount than invoiced. Instead, accounts payable requests a corrected invoice or a credit note from
the supplier.

### 5.2 Supplier communication
Communication with suppliers about discrepancies must be professional and factual. It must reference
the invoice number and the specific lines or amounts in question, state the expected and stated
amounts, and request a corrected invoice or credit note. It must not disclose internal approval
details, budget information or the names of internal approvers.

### 5.3 Escalation
Discrepancies or missing approvals that remain unresolved after 10 business days are escalated to
the finance controller.

## 6. Payment

### 6.1 Payment terms
Standard payment terms are 30 days net from the invoice issue date. Invoices on payment hold are not
paid until the hold is released, even if the due date has passed.

### 6.2 Early payment discounts
Early payment discounts offered by suppliers may only be used if approved by Treasury.

## 7. Automated decision support

### 7.1 Use of automated validation and AI assistants
Automated validation results and AI-generated explanations are decision support only. Final payment
and approval decisions are made by authorised finance staff. AI-generated text, including draft
emails to suppliers, must be reviewed by a person before it is sent externally.
